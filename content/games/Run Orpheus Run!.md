Run Orpheus Run! — TFS Game Jam, Summer 2025

In my first game jam, my team and I built an endless runner based on Greek mythology in 4 days. You play as Orpheus fleeing the Underworld, with Hades closing in behind you while navigating procedurally generated obstacles across three lanes, collect coins, and survive as long as possible before he catches you.

**My roles:**

**Lead Game Designer** — Defined the core gameplay loop, obstacle pacing, and difficulty scaling.

**UI Designer** — Designed and implemented the full UI, including HUD elements that communicate score and player state throughout the run.

**Audio Lead** — Composed and implemented the full audio experience, using sound to reinforce the urgency of the chase and the atmosphere of the Underworld.

The most challenging part of creating this game was truely understanding our time constraints and keep our project within scope. Luckily my team and I came to an agreement early on that we should only focus on having a single mechanic and build out from that. Although it was our first game jam and we lacked experience going into it, it taught us a lot about what it is like to work in a team with a tight deadline and the importance of keeping keeping a game simple, especially when you have absolutely ZERO experience in game dev.
